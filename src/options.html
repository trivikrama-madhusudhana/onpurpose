OnPurpose

Settings and usage

For desktop Chrome on YouTube. Your key stays in this browser profile. This extension does not run in the YouTube phone app.

You are ready to use YouTube

Open YouTube and enter the problem you want to solve in the OnPurpose bar beneath the search box. Choose Set goal to start.

Open YouTube

Already have YouTube open? Reload that tab once after installing or updating the extension. The toolbar popup also lets you set a goal and pause filtering.

Your OpenRouter key

Checking settings…

Saved only in this Chrome profile, never synced. Your key is used by the extension’s background service to call OpenRouter. It is not shared with YouTube.

Filtering sends your goal and video titles, channel names and available descriptions to OpenRouter and the Jev provider. Requests use your account’s credits. There is no extension analytics service.

Metadata can be incomplete or misleading. Videos classified as uncertain remain visible. You can pause filtering or reveal a collapsed video at any time.

Goal reminders

A reminder appears at a browsing break after this much time on off-topic videos. Useful and uncertain videos do not trigger reminders.
